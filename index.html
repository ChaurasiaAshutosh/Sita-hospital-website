About Us

Sita Super Speciality Hospital

Sita Super Speciality Hospital & Research Centre Pvt Ltd is a premier 100-bed multi-speciality hospital located at Main Road Birta-04, Birgunj. Founded in 2075 B.S. (2018 A.D.), our hospital is managed and operated under the guidance of renowned specialists Dr. Dilip Kumar Chaurasiya (Orthopaedics & Addiction Medicine) and Dr. Neelam Chaurasiya (Obs & Gynaecology).

Our motto — "We Treat, He Cures" — reflects our humble approach to healthcare. We believe in providing the highest quality medical services while maintaining compassion, dignity, and care for every patient.

From emergency services to specialized OPD consultations, from cutting-edge diagnostics to safe surgical procedures, Sita Hospital is committed to being the most trusted healthcare institution in the Madhesh region of Nepal.
